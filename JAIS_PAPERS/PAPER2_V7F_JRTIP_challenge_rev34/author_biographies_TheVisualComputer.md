Author biographies (50 to 100 words each, to be submitted with a black-and-white passport-size
photograph of each author)

Ahmed Gouda Ismail is an M.Sc. student in the Computer Engineering and Artificial Intelligence
Department at the Military Technical College, Cairo, Egypt. His research addresses real-time object
detection and multi-object tracking, with a focus on training-free methods that adapt detection and
tracking pipelines while they run. He is the first author of a survey on real-time object detection
and tracking presented at the 2026 IEEE International Conference on Machine Intelligence and Smart
Innovation (ICMISI 2026).

Mohamed S. Mohamed is an Associate Professor in the Computer Engineering and Artificial Intelligence
Department at the Military Technical College, Cairo, Egypt. He supervises graduate research in
computer engineering and artificial intelligence, including work on real-time object detection and
multi-object tracking for video analysis, and he is a co-author of the survey on real-time object
detection and tracking presented at the 2026 IEEE International Conference on Machine Intelligence
and Smart Innovation (ICMISI 2026).

Tarek Ahmed Mahmoud is a Professor in the Computer Engineering Department at Egypt University of
Informatics, Cairo, Egypt. He supervises graduate research in computer engineering and artificial
intelligence, including work on real-time object detection and multi-object tracking for video
analysis, and he is a co-author of the survey on real-time object detection and tracking presented
at the 2026 IEEE International Conference on Machine Intelligence and Smart Innovation (ICMISI 2026).
